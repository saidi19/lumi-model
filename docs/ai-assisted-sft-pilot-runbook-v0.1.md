# Lumi AI-assisted SFT pilot runbook v0.1

This is the active low-headcount route for the bounded 320-record behavior
pilot. It replaces the human-only packet workflow as the immediate gate, while
retaining that workflow as an optional higher-assurance track. It does not
authorize paid GPU work, production SFT, public release, or false human
authorship claims.

## Provenance rules

- Every generated row must say `human_written_from_scratch=false` and identify
  the generator. The current candidate was generated by the Codex session.
- Do not label Codex, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other model as a human
  author or independent human reviewer.
- Outputs from another model may be imported later only with explicit provider,
  model, generation method, rights, and review metadata.
- Raw candidate text stays in private Git-ignored `artifacts/`; only hashes,
  counts, and non-authorizing evidence may enter Git.

## Owner spot-check

The deterministic owner sample contains 32 rows: three train and one validation
row from each of the eight skills. Review naturalness, correctness, language,
identity/tool honesty, safety, and whether any repeated template pattern would
teach an undesirable habit. The owner may reject or request edits; no row is
silently promoted.

The private sample is at
`artifacts/ai-assisted-sft-pilot-v0.1/b4bb2f4e1cb3/attempt-003/owner-review-sample.jsonl`.
Its SHA-256-bound content fingerprint is
`bb30714e38df26497f875b0f927529041c5fad02419f60763b35067c21581ca1`.

Owner approval promotes only this small behavior candidate to reviewed input.
It still cannot serve as the complete SFT dataset: representative instruction,
coding, factual QA, writing, reasoning, and safety sources must be audited,
balanced, deduplicated, decontaminated, measured, and sealed first.

## Gate after owner review

After explicit owner signoff, create a signed raw-text-free review receipt,
update the source revision state without changing candidate text, and rerun the
source/filter audit. Then finish representative external-source audits and
measure the accepted task/language/token mix. Only a separately authorized,
production-eligible sealed dataset may advance to CPU seal-bound canaries and
later GPU SFT gates.
